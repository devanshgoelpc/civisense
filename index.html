ℹ️ About CiviSense AI Smart Civic Intelligence Platform

CiviSense AI converts a citizen's photograph of a civic problem into structured civic intelligence. The AI analyses the image, attaches GPS location, understands nearby context and surroundings, then calculates a priority score — turning raw citizen observations into actionable data for authorities. Repeated reports strengthen the same issue, and hotspot clustering reveals where recurring problems demand attention.
